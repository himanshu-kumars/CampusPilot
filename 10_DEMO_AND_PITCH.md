# CampusPilot — Hackathon Demo & Pitch

## 1. 30-Second Pitch

"College students manage attendance, assignments and exams across multiple places, but they rarely have one system telling them what matters right now. CampusPilot turns that scattered information into one personal academic command center. It tracks attendance, deadlines and exams, then uses AI to create a realistic study plan based on the student's actual preparation and available time."

## 2. Problem

Academic information is scattered.

The student must manually:
- track attendance
- remember deadlines
- calculate exam urgency
- decide what to study
- build a timetable

## 3. Solution

CampusPilot combines:
- attendance tracking
- assignment management
- exam planning
- AI personalization
- emergency study mode

## 4. Live Demo Script

### Step 1 — Dashboard
Show:
- 68% attendance in one subject
- 3 pending assignments
- exam in 5 days

Say:
"CampusPilot immediately tells me where I need attention."

### Step 2 — Attendance
Open the risky subject.

Say:
"Instead of only displaying 68%, it tells me how many consecutive classes I approximately need to attend to reach the target."

### Step 3 — Exam
Open Mathematics-I.

Say:
"I have five days left and only 30% preparation."

### Step 4 — AI Plan
Click Generate Study Plan.

Say:
"The AI is not generating a generic timetable. It uses my remaining days, available study time, preparation percentage and topics."

### Step 5 — Emergency Mode
Activate Emergency Study Mode.

Say:
"When time is limited, CampusPilot changes the strategy and prioritizes the most valuable work."

### Step 6 — Completion
Mark one task complete.

Say:
"The plan is actionable because the student can track completion instead of just reading an AI response."

## 5. Closing Statement

"CampusPilot doesn't replace a student's effort. It removes the planning chaos around that effort."

## 6. Judge Questions

### Why AI?
AI personalizes the plan to the student's real constraints instead of using a fixed timetable.

### Why not a normal task manager?
A normal task manager stores tasks. CampusPilot understands academic urgency, attendance and exam preparation together.

### What is innovative?
The strongest differentiator is the combination of academic state + time constraints + AI planning in one workflow, especially Emergency Study Mode.

### Is it scalable?
Yes. The architecture separates authentication, academic data, and AI services so future modules can be added independently.

### What next?
Notes/PDF understanding, AI question generation, viva practice, calendar integration and university integrations.
